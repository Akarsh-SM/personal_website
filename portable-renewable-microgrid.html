#Project Overview

IoT based Portable Renewable Microgrid

Designed and engineered the complete system architecture, hardware layout, and firmware codebases for a portable smart microgrid system that captures solar power, charges local battery cells, and regulates power routing dynamically.

Awards & Funding
  • Selected among the **Top 12 innovations** in Solar Decathlon India.
  • Virtually inaugurated by **Nitin Gadkari** (Union Minister for Road Transport & Highways) during the international conclave event.
  • Secured **Rs 3 Lakh** in direct government innovation funding through KITS (Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society) and NAIN (New Age Incubation Network).
Multilayer Analog & High-Speed PCB Layout
Real-time telemetry tracking (SPI, I2C, UART)
Dynamic load-management algorithm at the edge
Secure MQTT Cloud Synchronization

Technical Details

Target System Portable Renewable Energy Grid
Microcontrollers STM32 Cortex-M4, Espressif ESP32
Hardware Design 4-Layer High-Speed PCB Layout (Altium Designer)
Connectivity MQTT over Wi-Fi, RS485 Modbus Telemetry
Power Specs MPPT Solar Charger, LiFePO4 Battery Controller
Firmware Stack Embedded C, FreeRTOS scheduling, DMA bus configurations
